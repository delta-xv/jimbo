Theme music:
GBA arrangement by cellos51 and MeirGavish, based on the original Balatro
soundtrack by LouisF and transcription by MrCrimit.
https://louisfmusic.com/
https://musescore.com/user/8237321/scores/14590660
main_theme.xm rendered to stereo audio and encoded as Ogg Opus for Jimbo.
Jimbo also transcodes the Ogg file to 128 kbps AAC in an M4A container
for WebKit compatibility. The arrangement and musical content are unchanged.
